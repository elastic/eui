- Added `append` and `prepend` support to `EuiComboBox`.
- Added a `selectionDisplay` prop to `EuiComboBox`. The `collapse` value keeps selected options on a single line and shows overflow in a popover, whereas `wrap` lets the control grow to show every selection.
- Updated `EuiFormControlLayout` side dividers now stretch with multi-row controls and align with the first row when prepend or append content is present.
