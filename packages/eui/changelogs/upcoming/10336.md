- Added `append` and `prepend` support to multi-select `EuiComboBox` when `selectionDisplay` is explicitly set.
- Added a `selectionDisplay` prop to `EuiComboBox`. The `collapse` value keeps selected options on a single line and shows overflow in a popover, whereas `wrap` lets the control grow to show every selection.
- Updated `EuiFormControlLayout` side dividers to stretch across the full height of multi-row controls.
- Aligned `EuiComboBox` prepend and append content with the first row of a multi-row control.
